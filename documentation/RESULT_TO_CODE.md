Figure 1 and the source-excerpt portions of the worked example in Table 2 are not regenerated by this quantitative repository. Excerpt text and its private mapping are withheld. Event memberships, sequence links and numerical transition aggregation remain reproducible. Participant descriptive information not needed for the quantitative calculations is not added to the public input.

The integrated event-level table is rebuilt by `code/build_master_table.py` at stage `14b_master_table` and written to `outputs/integration/master_regime_table_eventlevel_REFIT.csv`. Its field definitions are in `documentation/MASTER_TABLE.md` and `provenance/MASTER_TABLE.json`.

The 13 extended publication tables are consolidated in `documentation/EXTENDED_RESULTS.md`. Main supplementary tables accompany the manuscript. The computational workflow generates the detailed analysis tables under `outputs/`; it does not require saved publication-table exports.
